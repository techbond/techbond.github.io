john-personal privacy policy

Last updated 15 October 2026.

john-personal is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

  • What it accesses: the owner's Gmail, Google Drive and Google Calendar, and only when the owner asks it to.
  • Where data goes: sign-in tokens are kept only on the owner's computer. There is no server for this app and no database. Data is read to answer the owner's own requests, including through the AI assistant the owner uses.
  • Sharing: data is not sold, shared with or transferred to anyone else, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models.
  • Google policy: use of data received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Revoking access: access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
